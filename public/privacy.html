Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Taro is a free web hosting and code editor. This page explains what information Taro stores and how it is used.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a password, handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see your password.
  • Profile: the name, username and profile photo you choose to add.
  • Projects: the files in your projects, their names, whether they are private or public, and counts such as likes, views and forks.
  • Activity: comments you post, and which projects you liked or disliked.
  • SQL databases: if you use SQL, your tables and data are stored in a private space that belongs only to your account.

What stays on your device

  • Your editor settings and unsaved drafts are kept in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Python code runs inside your browser and is not sent to our servers.

Code you run on the server

When you run Node.js code or SQL, your code is sent to our servers so it can run. Node.js files are written to a temporary folder, run, and deleted right after. The output is sent back to you.

Public projects

If you set a project to Public, anyone can view it, run it in the feed, and fork a copy. Your username and profile photo are shown next to public projects. Keep a project Private if you do not want it shared.

Services we rely on

Taro uses Google Firebase (sign-in and project storage), Render (servers), and Supabase (SQL databases). Your information is processed by these providers under their own policies. Taro also loads code libraries and fonts from public content delivery networks (such as cdnjs, jsDelivr and Google Fonts), which may see your IP address when your browser loads them.

Cleanup

SQL databases that are not used for a long time may be deleted automatically. Deleting a project removes its files. Back up anything important.

Your choices

  • You can edit your profile or delete your projects and comments inside Taro.
  • To ask for your account or data to be removed, contact us using the link below.

Children

Taro is not meant for children under 13. Please do not create an account if you are under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
